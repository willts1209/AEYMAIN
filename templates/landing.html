How it works

Upload the recording. Fieldcast transcribes UK bat species, then parses observations into a clean structured table.

Edit and export

Review any rows that need fixing. Copy the table straight into your Word report, or download as .docx.

Why Fieldcast

  • Faster than writing, more detail than memory. Two ecologists tested it against handwritten notes and confirmed the voice transcript captured more detail.
  • Built for UK species and survey workflows. 17 UK bat species, behaviours, vantage points, sunset times — recognised out of the box. No setup, no template-building.
  • Drops straight into your Word report. Copy a paste-ready table or export a .docx. Designed to feed your existing report template, not replace it.
  • Hands-busy by design. For surveys where clipboards don't work — emergence, tree PRA, hibernation, anywhere your hands are full.
